# Aura Finance AI - Monetization & Commerce Strategy

This document outlines the commercial structure of the Aura application, detailing what features are free and which are monetized across our subscription tiers.

## Subscription Tiers

| Feature / Limit | Starter (Free) | Growth (15k NGN/mo) | Enterprise (45k NGN/mo) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Target** | Startups & Solopreneurs | Growing SMEs | Multi-entity Corps |
| **Monthly Transactions** | 50 | 5,000 | Unlimited |
| **Monthly Invoices** | 10 | 500 | Unlimited |
| **Users** | Up to 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Bank Connections** | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |
| **AI CFO Access** | Basic (10 msgs) | Pro (500 msgs) | Unlimited |

---

## 1. Free Features (Starter Tier)
The "Starter" tier is designed to give every small business the foundation for digital bookkeeping without cost.

*   **Financial Dashboard:** Real-time visibility into cash flow and balances.
*   **Basic Bookkeeping:** Manual transaction entry and categorization.
*   **Accounts Receivable:** Standard invoicing for customer billing.
*   **Accounts Payable:** Recording and tracking vendor bills.
*   **Standard Reports:** Generating Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet statements.
*   **CRM (Contacts):** Managing basic customer and vendor profiles.
*   **Single Bank Sync:** Connecting one Nigerian bank account via Mono/Okra.

---

## 2. Monetized Features (Growth Tier)
The "Growth" tier introduces professional tools for businesses managing teams and physical products.

*   **Inventory Management:** SKU tracking, low-stock alerts, and multi-warehouse management.
*   **Automated Payroll:** Integrated payroll processing for up to 20 employees with automatic PAYE/Pension deductions.
*   **Tax Filing & Compliance:** Automated generation of VAT (7.5%), WHT, and SME CIT estimates.
*   **Estimates & Quotes:** Professional quotes that convert to invoices with one click.
*   **Project Accounting:** Tracking profitability, income, and expenses specifically for projects or departments.
*   **Smart Budgeting:** Planning monthly spending and tracking "Budget vs. Actual" performance.

---

## 3. Premium Monetized Features (Enterprise Tier)
The "Enterprise" tier is built for complex organizations and high-compliance environments.

*   **Multi-Entity Management:** Support for subsidiaries and branches with consolidated reporting.
*   **Fixed Asset Registry:** Automated straight-line or declining balance depreciation schedules.
*   **Compliance Audit Trail:** Permanent history of all financial changes for SOC2 and audit readiness.
*   **Year-End Closing Wizard:** Guided tools for closing the fiscal year and locking books.
*   **Advanced AI CFO Brain:** Priority access to predictive cash flow reasoning and anomaly detection.
*   **Custom API Access:** Programmatic access for external integrations.

---

## Usage-Based Monetization (Add-ons)
While tiers have limits, businesses can purchase "Power Packs" if they exceed their monthly quota without wanting to move to the next tier:
*   **AI Pack:** 1,000 additional messages.
*   **OCR Pack:** 100 additional receipt scans.
*   **Sync Pack:** Additional bank connections beyond the tier limit.
