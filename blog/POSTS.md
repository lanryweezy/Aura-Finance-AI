## Post 2: "How to Calculate PAYE, Pension, and NHF in Nigeria (2026 Guide)"

**SEO Keywords:** PAYE calculator Nigeria, pension contribution Nigeria, NHF calculation, Nigerian payroll

Running payroll in Nigeria isn't just about paying salaries. You need to calculate:

- **PAYE (Pay As You Earn)** — Progressive tax from 7% to 24%
- **Pension** — 10% of gross salary (employer + employee)
- **NHF (National Housing Fund)** — 2.5% of gross salary
- **NSITF** — 1% of gross salary (employer only)

Here's the formula:

1. **Gross Salary** = Basic + Housing + Transport + Allowances
2. **Consolidated Relief Allowance** = ₦200,000 + 20% of gross
3. **Pension Relief** = 8% of gross (max ₦50,000/month)
4. **Taxable Income** = Gross - CRA - Pension Relief
5. **PAYE** = Apply progressive rates to taxable income
6. **Net Salary** = Gross - PAYE - Pension - NHF

Example for ₦500,000 gross:
- CRA: ₦200,000 + ₦100,000 = ₦300,000
- Pension Relief: ₦40,000
- Taxable: ₦500,000 - ₦300,000 - ₦40,000 = ₦160,000
- PAYE: ~₦15,200
- Pension: ₦50,000
- NHF: ₦12,500
- **Net: ~₦422,300**

## Post 3: "NRS E-Invoicing: What Every Nigerian Business Must Know Before July 2026"

**SEO Keywords:** NRS e-invoicing Nigeria, FIRS e-invoicing, digital invoice Nigeria, tax compliance 2026

The Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) has mandated electronic invoicing for all businesses. Here's what you need to know:

### What is NRS E-Invoicing?
Every invoice must be:
1. Generated electronically (not scanned paper)
2. Validated by NRS in real-time
3. Digitally stamped with a Cryptographic Stamp Identifier (CSID)
4. Transmitted through an Access Point Provider (APP)

### Who Must Comply?
- **Large taxpayers** (>₦5B turnover): Already required since July 2025
- **Medium taxpayers** (₦1B-₦5B): Required from July 1, 2026
- **Small taxpayers**: Expected 2027

### What Happens If You Don't?
- ₦200,000 administrative fine
- ₦10,000/day penalty
- 100% tax surcharge on unreported transactions
- Input VAT becomes non-claimable
