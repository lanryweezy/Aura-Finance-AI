## Case Study 1: TechFlow Lagos — Replaced 4 Tools with Aura

**Company:** TechFlow Lagos (Tech startup, 15 employees)
**Challenge:** Using 4 separate tools for invoicing, expenses, payroll, and accounting. Cost: ₦180,000/month total.

**Solution:** Migrated to Aura Finance AI (Growth plan — ₦15,000/month).

**Results:**
- **87% cost reduction** — ₦180K → ₦15K/month
- **10 hours/month saved** — AI CFO handles financial analysis
- **3x faster invoicing** — WhatsApp sharing + AI extraction
- **NRS compliant** — E-invoicing ready before deadline

**Quote:** "Aura replaced 4 tools we were paying for. The AI CFO alone saves us 10 hours every month." — Adaeze O., CEO
